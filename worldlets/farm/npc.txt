!farm/npc/owner!
parent = "farm/generic_npc"
name = "a sunburnt woman in a flour-dusted apron"
location = "farm/room/road"
tick = 60
description = Description("""
A stocky woman in her middle years, her face and forearms
deeply tanned from long days outdoors. Her hands are rough
and calloused, dusted white with flour up to the wrists.
A faded cotton apron, once blue, is knotted tightly over
a patched linen dress. Her dark hair is pulled back under
a kerchief, though a few loose strands cling to her damp
forehead. Her eyes are red-rimmed, and she keeps glancing
south toward the farm gate as if expecting someone small
to come running back through it.
""")

def on_tick(self):
    messages = [
        f"{self} wrings her flour-dusted hands, pacing in a tight circle.",
        f"{self} shades her eyes and stares down the road, lips pressed thin.",
        f"{self} draws a sharp breath and steadies herself against the gatepost.",
        f"{self} mutters something under her breath and clutches her apron.",
    ]

    self.location.announce(random.choice(messages), prompt=False)

def enter(self, character: Entity):
    """Called when a character enters the NPC's room."""
    message = f"""
        {self} catches sight of you and hurries over, breathless:

        "Please -- have you come from the south road? My boy, Tom,
        he went out to the fields this morning and hasn't come back.
        He's only seven. I've been calling and calling but the gate's
        rusted shut and I can't get through. The whole farm's gone
        wrong today -- the lock on the house is jammed, the stairs
        are half rotten, and Bramble's still chained up in the barn.

        If you could get through the gate and find him, I'd be
        grateful beyond words. He'll be out past the barn somewhere,
        I'm sure of it. Please hurry."
    """

    character.msg(message)

name = "a small boy sitting in the wheat"

A wiry boy of about seven, with a mess of sun-bleached
hair and a face full of freckles. His knees are grass-stained
and his bare feet are caked with dry mud. He wears an
oversized linen shirt that hangs past his elbows, the sleeves
rolled up in thick, uneven folds. A piece of straw is tucked
behind one ear, and he's turning a smooth pebble over and
over in his fingers, watching it catch the light.
""")
location = "farm/room/fields"

def enter(self, character: Entity):
    """When the character enters the room."""
    message = f"""
        {self} looks up with a start and squints at you, shielding
        his eyes from the sun.

        "Oh. You're not Mum." He turns the pebble in his hand
        and frowns. "She's going to be cross, isn't she? I only
        came out to watch the skylark. Then the gate got stuck
        and I couldn't get back. Is Bramble alright?"
    """

    character.msg(message)
