# Gold coins stack: 30 on the floor, take 20, and the rest stays behind.

given Alice in !room/bakery!
given 30 !object/gold_coin! in !room/bakery!

Alice> look
Alice sees "30 gold coins"

Alice> get 20 gold coin
Alice sees "20 gold coins"

Alice> look
Alice sees "10 gold coins"
Alice does not see "30 gold coins"

# The room says "gold coins", so that is what a player types back.
Alice> get 5 gold coins
Alice sees "5 gold coins"

Alice> drop 25 gold coins
Alice sees "25 gold coins"

Alice> look
Alice sees "30 gold coins"

# The plural finds a single coin too.
Alice> get 1 gold coin
Alice> drop gold coins
Alice sees "a gold coin"
